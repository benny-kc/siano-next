❓ How to use Siano

Siano splits a group trip's bills. Everyone who opens the trip's link sees the same live board — share it from ⚙️ Settings.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Travellers

  • Add or remove travellers in ⚙️ Settings.
  • Drag a traveller onto a meal card to add them to that bill.
  • Drag one onto empty board space to start a new meal with them.

🍽️ Meals & bills

  • Add a card with ➕ (top), or by dropping a traveller on empty space.
  • Move a card by dragging the ⠿ grip or its emoji.
  • Rename: tap the title. Total: tap the amount and type.
  • Who paid: tap a person's round chip → 💳 marks the payer.
  • Exact share: press & hold a person's name to type their amount (📌 = custom).
  • ✕ Close keeps the bill in history; 🗑 Delete removes it.

🖐️ Moving the board

  • One finger on empty space (or a card's blank body) drags the board.
  • Two fingers pan; pinch to zoom.
  • Rotate your phone — whatever was centred stays centred.

💰 Shared budgets

  • In Settings, set a traveller to “shared with …” to pool money (e.g. a couple).
  • Meals still split per person, but who owes whom is settled per budget.

📂 Drawers

  • Bills history: the ☰ button, or swipe in from the left edge.
  • Settings: the ⚙️ button, or swipe in from the right edge.
  • The phone's Back button closes an open drawer.

Everything here is also learnable by tapping around — nothing destructive happens without a confirmation. Come back any time via ⚙️ Settings → ❓.
